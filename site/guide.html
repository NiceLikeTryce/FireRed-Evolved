FIRERED EVOLVED + EVOLV3D

Your adventure starts here.

Both editions share the expanded FireRed adventure. Pick the presentation you prefer, then keep your save as you update.

Build and play

  1. Open the patcher hub and choose GBA or Nintendo 3DS.
  2. Select a clean US/English FireRed v1.0 or v1.1 ROM. The revision is detected automatically.
  3. Build and download your game.

GBA: open the .gba in a GBA emulator. Keep its battery save alongside it with the same filename.

3DS: open the .3dsx in Azahar, or copy it into /3ds/FireRedEvolved/ on the SD card and open it through Homebrew Launcher.

Save, update, continue

Save from the in-game menu, then close the game before replacing files. Build an update from the original clean ROM rather than an already-patched game.

On GBA, match the save filename to the new .gba filename. On 3DS, keep /3ds/FireRedEvolved/game.sav. Always keep a backup of that folder before updating.

The save transfer tool prepares the right filename and includes an untouched backup when moving between editions. Emulator save states are separate from battery saves.

Pokédex rewards

Mew: catch every species #001–150 for the truck event.

Celebi: catch every species #001–250 for Oak’s GS Ball and Faraway Island reward. Celebi itself is excluded from that requirement.

Gen 3 catches do not count toward either reward. Gen 3 support follows the normal postgame Ruby and Sapphire quest.
