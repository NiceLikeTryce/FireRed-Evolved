Use the 128 KiB save file, not a quick-save or emulator save state. Original US/English FireRed v1.0/v1.1 saves are supported. The tool checks integrity; saves do not identify their ROM revision. Files stay in your browser.

After downloading

  1. Close the game and back up its current save.
  2. Extract the ZIP. It contains your transferred save, an untouched backup and instructions.
  3. For 3DS, copy game.sav into /3ds/FireRedEvolved/ on the SD card or emulator SD folder.
  4. Start the game and choose Continue.

Party, storage, items, story progress and supported extra game data move together. 3DS settings, randomizer settings and tracker sidecars stay with the 3DS installation.
